About us

A game for friends, dreamed up by a father and his son.

Raph, the creator

The idea is Raph's: every WhatsApp group has someone who always says the same thing, someone who sends the same sticker, someone who never replies. He turned it into a party game where everyone guesses who said what, each on their own phone. He came up with all of it and coded all of it.

When he's not making games, Raph raps as FAR.

Stéphane, his dad

Stéphane holds a PhD in artificial intelligence and is an entrepreneur. With Cambon AI, he helps small and mid-sized companies tackle their hardest problems (optimizing, forecasting, targeting, automating), the way a large company's R&D team would.

On Who is bro ?, he simply made sure your data stays protected, gave a few tips to make the game easier to pick up, and bought the whoisbro.lol domain (€1.80 for the whole year, no less!).
